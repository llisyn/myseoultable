Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy covers My Seoul Table Uploader, a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel My Seoul Table (@MySeoulTable) to upload and schedule the owner's own videos.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uses the YouTube Data API to: upload the owner's own videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel, set their titles, descriptions, tags and scheduled publish time, and read the owner's basic channel information to confirm uploads go to the correct channel.

Scopes used: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, or process data about any other YouTube users or viewers.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Data storage

The OAuth refresh token and a local log of the owner's own uploaded video IDs, titles and scheduled times are stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
